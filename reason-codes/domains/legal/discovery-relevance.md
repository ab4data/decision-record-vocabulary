# Document discovery relevance

**Sector:** legal

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `relevant_and_produced` | `conformed` | Within the agreed scope and produced |
| `document_incomplete` | `evidence_insufficient` | Fragment or attachment missing |
| `metadata_conflicts` | `evidence_contradicts` | Metadata inconsistent with the document content |
| `privileged` | `rule_conflict` | Legal professional privilege claimed |
| `outside_agreed_scope` | `out_of_scope` | Outside the date range or custodian list |
| `proportionality` | `capacity_or_cost` | Excluded on review cost against likely value |
| `client_instruction` | `third_party` | Withheld on instruction, recorded |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
