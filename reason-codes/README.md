# Starter reason-code lists

**Six to eight values, written by the people who do the work.** These are starting points to argue with, not a standard — the point is that *your* domain writes *its* list.

Three rules that matter more than the lists themselves:

1. ⭐ **Six to eight values.** Fewer and it says nothing; more and nobody picks accurately.
2. ⭐ **Always include `other`, with an optional free-text field** — and treat its rate as a monitored metric. A climbing "other" rate means the list has stopped fitting, or people have stopped caring.
3. **Revise the list on a schedule.** A list written once and never revisited is how the rows stop meaning anything.

**Add your domain by opening a pull request.** One file, one table, same shape. That is the single most useful contribution anyone can make here.
