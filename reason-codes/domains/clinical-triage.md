# Clinical triage — emergency presentation

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `criteria_met` | conformed | Acuity scored against the standard and allocated accordingly |
| `obs_incomplete` | evidence_insufficient | Required observations missing or not recorded in time |
| `history_conflicts` | evidence_contradicts | Presentation does not match the reported history |
| `comorbidity_uplift` | risk_elevated | Scored higher because of coexisting conditions |
| `deterioration_risk` | risk_elevated | Stable now, but trajectory drove the decision |
| `outside_service_scope` | out_of_scope | Needs a service this site does not provide |
| `senior_review_required` | authority_exceeded | Beyond the triage nurse's scope of practice |
| `capacity_constrained` | capacity_or_cost | Allocation shaped by bed or staff availability |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
