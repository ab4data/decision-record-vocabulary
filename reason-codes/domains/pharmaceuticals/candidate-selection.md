# Molecule candidate progression

**Sector:** pharmaceuticals

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `progressed_to_development` | `conformed` | Meets the target product profile and the progression criteria |
| `scale_up_data_missing` | `evidence_insufficient` | No demonstrated route at the scale the programme needs |
| `tox_signal_conflicts` | `evidence_contradicts` | Safety findings disagree across models or studies |
| `selectivity_below_threshold` | `rule_conflict` | Fails a defined selectivity or safety margin |
| `cost_of_goods_prohibitive` | `capacity_or_cost` | Projected manufacturing cost exceeds the target |
| `portfolio_slot_unavailable` | `capacity_or_cost` | Sound candidate, no development capacity this cycle |
| `freedom_to_operate` | `third_party` | Patent position held by another party |
| `committee_referral` | `authority_exceeded` | Beyond the team progression authority |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
