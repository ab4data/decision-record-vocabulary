# Performance rating moderation

**Sector:** people

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `rating_confirmed` | `conformed` | Manager rating upheld at moderation |
| `evidence_thin` | `evidence_insufficient` | Insufficient documented examples to support the rating |
| `rating_vs_evidence` | `evidence_contradicts` | Rating inconsistent with the recorded evidence |
| `distribution_guidance` | `rule_conflict` | Adjusted against distribution guidance |
| `consistency_across_teams` | `risk_elevated` | Adjusted for comparability rather than performance |
| `part_year_or_leave` | `timing` | Period of service shaped the outcome |
| `escalated_hr` | `authority_exceeded` | Referred for review |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
