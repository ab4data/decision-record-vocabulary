# Human research ethics review

**Sector:** research

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `approved_as_submitted` | `conformed` | Meets the national statement without modification |
| `protocol_detail_missing` | `evidence_insufficient` | Method or recruitment detail insufficient to assess |
| `consent_inconsistent` | `evidence_contradicts` | Participant materials conflict with the protocol |
| `risk_benefit_concern` | `risk_elevated` | Burden to participants not justified by the benefit |
| `privacy_or_data_handling` | `rule_conflict` | Data management breaches a requirement |
| `vulnerable_participants` | `rule_conflict` | Additional safeguards required by the category |
| `outside_committee_remit` | `out_of_scope` | Low risk, or another committee responsibility |
| `conflict_declared` | `authority_exceeded` | Member stood aside, decided by the remaining panel |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
