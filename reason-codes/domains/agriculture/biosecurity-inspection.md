# Biosecurity inspection decision

**Sector:** agriculture

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `cleared_on_inspection` | `conformed` | The consignment met the import or movement conditions |
| `certificate_incomplete` | `evidence_insufficient` | Phytosanitary or health certificate incomplete |
| `declaration_conflicts` | `evidence_contradicts` | Declared contents differ from what was found |
| `prohibited_material` | `rule_conflict` | The item is on a prohibited or restricted list |
| `pest_risk_elevated` | `risk_elevated` | Held on assessed pest or disease risk |
| `treatment_capacity` | `capacity_or_cost` | No fumigation or treatment slot available |
| `perishable_window` | `timing` | Spoilage time shaped the decision |
| `exporting_authority` | `third_party` | Determined by the exporting country authority |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
