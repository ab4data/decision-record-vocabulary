# Contract review

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `approved_on_standard_terms` | conformed | Matches the approved template |
| `instructions_unclear` | evidence_insufficient | Commercial intent not stated well enough to advise |
| `terms_conflict_with_brief` | evidence_contradicts | Drafting does not reflect what was agreed |
| `liability_outside_policy` | rule_conflict | Cap, indemnity or insurance outside policy |
| `jurisdiction_or_law` | rule_conflict | Governing law or forum unacceptable |
| `commercial_risk_accepted` | risk_elevated | Signed with a known risk, consciously accepted |
| `counterparty_refused` | third_party | Position held by the other side |
| `delegation_exceeded` | authority_exceeded | Above the signing authority |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
