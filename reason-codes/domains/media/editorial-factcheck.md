# Editorial fact-check decision

**Sector:** media

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `verified_to_standard` | `conformed` | Claims verified against the editorial standard |
| `sourcing_insufficient` | `evidence_insufficient` | Single source or unverifiable attribution |
| `sources_disagree` | `evidence_contradicts` | Credible sources materially disagree |
| `legal_risk` | `rule_conflict` | Defamation or contempt risk identified |
| `public_interest_override` | `risk_elevated` | Published with known residual uncertainty |
| `deadline_pressure` | `timing` | Published or held against the deadline |
| `subject_response_pending` | `third_party` | Awaiting a right of reply |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
