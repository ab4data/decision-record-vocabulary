# Procurement evaluation

**Sector:** government

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `highest_value_compliant` | `conformed` | Best value among conforming offers |
| `response_incomplete` | `evidence_insufficient` | Mandatory schedules not provided |
| `claims_unsubstantiated` | `evidence_contradicts` | Capability claims not supported by references |
| `non_conforming_offer` | `rule_conflict` | Departs from mandatory requirements |
| `price_outside_budget` | `capacity_or_cost` | Technically sound, unaffordable |
| `supplier_risk` | `risk_elevated` | Financial or delivery risk drove the decision |
| `probity_issue` | `authority_exceeded` | Referred because of a probity concern |
| `late_submission` | `timing` | Received after the close of tenders |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
