# Radiology report sign-off

**Sector:** health

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `agrees_with_report` | `conformed` | Findings and report agree, signed as drafted |
| `study_technically_limited` | `evidence_insufficient` | Positioning, motion or coverage limited the read |
| `discrepancy_with_prior` | `evidence_contradicts` | Differs from a previous study without explanation |
| `incidental_finding` | `risk_elevated` | Report amended for a finding outside the clinical question |
| `urgent_communication` | `rule_conflict` | Direct notification mandated by the finding |
| `priors_unavailable` | `evidence_insufficient` | Comparison not possible |
| `second_read_requested` | `authority_exceeded` | Referred for subspecialty opinion |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
