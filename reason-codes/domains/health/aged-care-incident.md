# Aged care incident assessment

**Sector:** health

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `managed_under_care_plan` | `conformed` | Incident handled as the care plan anticipates |
| `witness_unavailable` | `evidence_insufficient` | No staff or resident account obtainable |
| `accounts_conflict` | `evidence_contradicts` | Resident and staff accounts differ materially |
| `reportable_incident` | `rule_conflict` | Meets the statutory reporting threshold |
| `recurrence_pattern` | `risk_elevated` | Rated higher because the pattern is repeating |
| `staffing_level_factor` | `capacity_or_cost` | Rostering contributed to the circumstances |
| `family_complaint` | `third_party` | Assessment driven by an external complaint |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
