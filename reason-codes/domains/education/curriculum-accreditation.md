# Course accreditation decision

**Sector:** education

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `meets_standards` | `conformed` | Programme meets the accreditation standards |
| `evidence_of_outcomes_thin` | `evidence_insufficient` | Insufficient evidence that outcomes are achieved |
| `mapping_inconsistent` | `evidence_contradicts` | Stated outcomes do not match assessment design |
| `standard_not_met` | `rule_conflict` | A specific standard is unmet |
| `staffing_capability` | `capacity_or_cost` | Teaching capacity insufficient for the proposal |
| `conditional_accreditation` | `risk_elevated` | Approved with conditions and review |
| `professional_body_view` | `third_party` | Determined with an external accrediting body |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
