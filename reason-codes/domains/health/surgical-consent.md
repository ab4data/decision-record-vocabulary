# Surgical consent review

**Sector:** health

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `consent_valid` | `conformed` | Capacity, information and voluntariness all satisfied |
| `risks_not_documented` | `evidence_insufficient` | Specific risks not recorded as discussed |
| `understanding_unclear` | `evidence_contradicts` | Patient account differs from the documented discussion |
| `capacity_assessment_required` | `authority_exceeded` | Referred for a formal capacity assessment |
| `interpreter_required` | `rule_conflict` | Consent invalid without an accredited interpreter |
| `substitute_decision_maker` | `third_party` | Decision rests with a guardian or nominee |
| `procedure_changed` | `timing` | Consent predates a change in the planned procedure |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
