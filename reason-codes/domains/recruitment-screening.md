# Recruitment screening

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `meets_all_essential` | conformed | Meets every essential criterion |
| `application_incomplete` | evidence_insufficient | Required responses or documents missing |
| `claims_unsupported` | evidence_contradicts | Experience claimed is not evidenced in the history |
| `essential_criterion_unmet` | rule_conflict | Fails a stated essential requirement |
| `not_eligible_to_work` | out_of_scope | Work rights, registration or licence absent |
| `stronger_field` | capacity_or_cost | Capable, and ranked below others for limited places |
| `withdrew_or_unavailable` | timing | No longer available for the role |
| `panel_referral` | authority_exceeded | Beyond the screener's discretion |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
