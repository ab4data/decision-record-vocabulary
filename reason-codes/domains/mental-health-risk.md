# Mental health risk assessment

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `risk_rated_to_tool` | conformed | Rating follows the structured instrument |
| `collateral_unavailable` | evidence_insufficient | Could not obtain family, GP or service history |
| `account_inconsistent` | evidence_contradicts | Self-report conflicts with observed presentation |
| `protective_factors` | risk_elevated | Rated lower because of supports in place |
| `recent_escalation` | risk_elevated | Rated higher on recent change rather than static score |
| `clinical_override` | authority_exceeded | Clinician departed from the tool, with reasons |
| `legal_status_applies` | rule_conflict | Involuntary provisions shaped the decision |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
