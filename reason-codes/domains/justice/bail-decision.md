# Bail determination

**Sector:** justice

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `released_on_conditions` | `conformed` | Standard conditions assessed as sufficient |
| `antecedents_unavailable` | `evidence_insufficient` | Criminal history or address not verified in time |
| `submissions_conflict` | `evidence_contradicts` | Prosecution and defence accounts differ materially |
| `unacceptable_risk` | `risk_elevated` | Refused on assessed risk of reoffending or flight |
| `show_cause_offence` | `rule_conflict` | Statutory presumption applied |
| `no_suitable_accommodation` | `capacity_or_cost` | Supported accommodation unavailable |
| `adjourned_for_report` | `timing` | Decision deferred pending an assessment |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
