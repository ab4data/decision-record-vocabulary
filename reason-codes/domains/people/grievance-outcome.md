# Workplace grievance outcome

**Sector:** people

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `not_substantiated` | `conformed` | Investigated, allegation not substantiated |
| `witnesses_unavailable` | `evidence_insufficient` | Key witnesses declined or unavailable |
| `accounts_conflict` | `evidence_contradicts` | Direct conflict between accounts, no corroboration |
| `policy_breach_found` | `rule_conflict` | A specific policy provision breached |
| `safety_or_wellbeing` | `risk_elevated` | Interim measures taken on assessed risk |
| `external_process` | `third_party` | Matter before an external body |
| `investigation_timeframe` | `timing` | Outcome shaped by the required timeframe |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
