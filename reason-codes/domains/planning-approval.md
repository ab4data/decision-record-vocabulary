# Planning and development approval

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `complies_with_controls` | conformed | Meets the planning scheme without variation |
| `documents_inadequate` | evidence_insufficient | Plans or reports missing or not to standard |
| `survey_conflicts` | evidence_contradicts | Submitted plans conflict with survey or site inspection |
| `variation_within_discretion` | rule_conflict | Departs from a control, allowed under discretion |
| `prohibited_in_zone` | out_of_scope | Use is not permissible in the zone |
| `amenity_impact` | risk_elevated | Overshadowing, traffic or noise drove the decision |
| `referral_authority` | third_party | Determined by a referral body's response |
| `submission_period` | timing | Public exhibition timing shaped the outcome |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
