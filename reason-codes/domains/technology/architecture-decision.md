# Architecture decision record

**Sector:** technology

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `accepted` | `conformed` | Option selected and recorded against the drivers |
| `options_not_evaluated` | `evidence_insufficient` | Alternatives not assessed in enough detail |
| `assumptions_contradicted` | `evidence_contradicts` | A stated assumption proved wrong in a spike |
| `constraint_breach` | `rule_conflict` | Conflicts with a standard or a prior decision |
| `supersedes_prior` | `rule_conflict` | Replaces an earlier decision, which is marked superseded |
| `operational_risk` | `risk_elevated` | Chosen for resilience over other qualities |
| `licence_or_cost` | `capacity_or_cost` | Driven by licensing or run cost |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
