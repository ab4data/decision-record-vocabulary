# Change advisory board decision

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `approved_as_submitted` | conformed | Meets the change standard; approved unchanged |
| `plan_incomplete` | evidence_insufficient | Backout or test evidence missing |
| `test_results_conflict` | evidence_contradicts | Results do not support the stated readiness |
| `window_conflict` | timing | Clashes with a freeze or another change |
| `risk_requires_pilot` | risk_elevated | Approved only as a staged rollout |
| `dependency_unresolved` | third_party | Blocked on another team or supplier |
| `standard_change` | rule_conflict | Pre-authorised; no board decision needed |
| `emergency_authority` | authority_exceeded | Approved outside the board under emergency powers |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
