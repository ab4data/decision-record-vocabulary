what that one column buys you

A reported number turns out to be wrong.
Which decisions did it actually change?

Each judged attempt is a node. Each thing it cited is a link to an earlier one. Follow the links and you have the answer — for a bad reported number, a withdrawn policy, a model you no longer trust.

A decision can rest on a reported number, a document, or an earlier decision — that is precedent. Every chain ends, because nothing can cite what did not yet exist. Follow one down and you reach an observation, a measured rate, or an assertion. A rule promoted off a measured rate can be re-run next quarter. One that was simply decided cannot — and the proportions tell you how much of your doctrine is evidence.

The question the last wave could not answer, and this one can.
