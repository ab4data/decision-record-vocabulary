# Benefits and entitlement eligibility

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `qualifies_on_evidence` | conformed | Meets every eligibility test on the evidence supplied |
| `evidence_not_provided` | evidence_insufficient | Requested documents not received |
| `declared_vs_recorded` | evidence_contradicts | Declaration conflicts with other agency records |
| `income_or_asset_test` | rule_conflict | Fails a means test |
| `residency_or_status` | out_of_scope | Residency, age or status requirement not met |
| `claim_lodged_late` | timing | Backdating rules changed the entitlement |
| `change_of_circumstance` | rule_conflict | Entitlement changed mid-period |
| `referred_review_officer` | authority_exceeded | Beyond the assessor's delegation |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
