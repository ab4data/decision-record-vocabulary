# Insurance underwriting

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `standard_terms` | conformed | Accepted at standard rates, no loading |
| `disclosure_incomplete` | evidence_insufficient | Material questions unanswered |
| `disclosure_inconsistent` | evidence_contradicts | Application conflicts with medical or claims history |
| `risk_loaded` | risk_elevated | Accepted with a premium loading |
| `exclusion_applied` | rule_conflict | Accepted with a specific exclusion |
| `outside_appetite` | out_of_scope | Occupation, asset or geography outside appetite |
| `aggregation_limit` | capacity_or_cost | Portfolio concentration drove the decision |
| `referred_reinsurer` | authority_exceeded | Beyond the retained authority |
| `other` | other | Free text required |
