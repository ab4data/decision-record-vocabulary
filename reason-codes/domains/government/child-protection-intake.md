# Child protection intake decision

**Sector:** government

| `code` | `class` | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `below_threshold` | `conformed` | Concern assessed and did not meet the statutory threshold |
| `reporter_detail_limited` | `evidence_insufficient` | Notifier could not provide identifying or specific detail |
| `records_conflict` | `evidence_contradicts` | Reported concern differs from case history |
| `cumulative_harm` | `risk_elevated` | Escalated on pattern rather than single incident |
| `mandatory_response_time` | `timing` | Response window set by the assessed category |
| `family_already_engaged` | `third_party` | Another agency holds the active case |
| `referred_senior_practitioner` | `authority_exceeded` | Beyond the intake worker decision |
| `other` | `other` | Free text required |
